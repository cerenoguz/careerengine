01 / PRODUCT FLOW

A daily decision system, not another job board.

CareerEngine re-evaluates jobs observed during each scan rather than permanently hiding them after one appearance. It surfaces the strongest current matches while making clear which postings are newly discovered.

01

Collect

Pulls public postings from Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever, and Workable, plus dedicated collectors for large employers outside those platforms.

02

Evaluate

Removes internships, off-target grad years, and clear authorization mismatches — then screens for companies with a real, verified visa-sponsorship track record.

03

Rank

Combines explainable technical-fit signals with bounded Sentence-BERT profile alignment.

04

Deliver

Emails the top newly discovered matches, ranked best-fit first, with everything else ranked and waiting in the dashboard.

04 / ENGINEERING DECISIONS

Hybrid ranking without an opaque black box.

Deterministic logic handles clear restrictions such as citizenship, clearance, and required experience. Semantic similarity contributes a limited, inspectable signal rather than making the final decision alone.

Explainable filtering

Evidence-based work-authorization and seniority checks keep hard constraints explicit.

Visa-aware by design

Companies are screened against real H-1B filing history, not assumed — and removed the moment that track record doesn’t hold up.

Profile-aware ranking

Sentence-BERT measures semantic alignment with a technical profile while rule-based scoring preserves control.

Fresh daily ranking

Current jobs are re-ranked every scan; first-observed dates distinguish new postings from existing matches.
